Why Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Wins

Discover why plant-engineered RMC outperforms traditional site-mixed concrete across key parameters.

1. Precision & Quality

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Fully automated computerized batching plant controls precise water-cement ratio and aggregate grading (IS 456 compliant).

Conventional Site-Mix

Manual volumetric mixing on-site leads to high strength variation, improper ratios, and human error.

2. Speed & Pour Efficiency

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Delivered ready-to-pour by transit mixers. Continuous pouring cuts slab completion time by more than 50%.

Conventional Site-Mix

Slow manual batching extends pour duration, risking cold joints, weak spots, and project delays.

3. Durability & Compressive Strength

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Guaranteed cube strength verified through lab testing, optimal compaction, and long structural durability.

Conventional Site-Mix

High moisture variation causes segregation, honeycomb formation, micro-cracks, and reduced durability.

4. Site Space & Cleanliness

RMC (Namma Concrete)

No need to stockpile raw materials at the construction site. Keeps your site clean, spacious, and dust-free.

Conventional Site-Mix

Large stockpiles of sand, aggregate, and cement bags must be stored on-site, obstructing site access and causing dust pollution.

5. Labor Efficiency & Safety

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Minimal labor required on pour day. Transit mixers and concrete pumps handle heavy discharge with maximum safety.

Conventional Site-Mix

Requires a large number of laborers for mixing and transporting, increasing labor cost, management burden, and safety risks.

6. Material Wastage & Cost Control

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Order exactly the cubic meter (m³) quantity you need, with zero material wastage, accurate pricing, and lower overall costs.

Conventional Site-Mix

10–15% material loss occurs due to spillage, rain damage, cement bags hardening, theft, and inaccurate on-site mixing.

7. Environmental Impact

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Eco-friendly plant operations with wet-dust collectors, controlled noise levels, and optimized water conservation.

Conventional Site-Mix

Airborne cement dust pollution at the worksite, high noise levels, and slurry runoff into nearby areas without proper management.

8. Testing & Quality Certification

RMC (Namma Concrete)

Computerized batch dockets, slump verification, and certified 7-day and 28-day cube test reports for structural compliance.

Conventional Site-Mix

No batching records, no lab test reports, and unverified water-cement ratios — everything relies purely on visual guesswork.

Terms & Conditions - Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC)

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Quotations & Order Acceptance

All Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) orders placed with Namma Concrete are subject to batching plant capacity, raw material availability, and final delivery schedule confirmation. Volume calculations in cubic meters (m³) are based on customer specifications. Any variation on site during pouring will be charged accordingly.

2. Site Access & Unloading Facilities

The purchaser must ensure a safe, unobstructed, and sturdy access route for transit mixers (up to 30 tonnes gross weight) and concrete boom pumps from the main road to the discharge point. Namma Concrete is not responsible for site road damage or mixer entrapment due to inadequate site preparation. Demurrage charges may apply if discharge exceeds 45 minutes per transit mixer.

3. Quality Assurance & Standards (IS 456 / IS 4926)

Our concrete is batched using computerized automated controls compliant with Indian Standard specifications (IS 456 & IS 4926). Any addition of water, site-mixed cement, or unauthorized admixtures by site personnel without written authorization from our quality engineer voids all slump and compressive strength warranties.

4. Sampling, Testing & Cube Strength

Concrete cube specimens sampled at the transit mixer discharge point in the presence of site representatives will serve as official test samples. 7-day and 28-day compressive strength reports generated by Namma Concrete certified laboratory are final. Joint sampling procedures must follow IS 1199 protocols.

5. Payment Terms & Billing

Unless credit terms have been pre-approved in writing, full payment must be settled prior to truck dispatch. Payments are non-refundable once batching has commenced. Applicable GST and transportation tariffs are itemized on official tax invoices.

6. Delivery Schedules & Force Majeure

Namma Concrete will make every effort to maintain agreed delivery time slots. However, we are not liable for delays caused by traffic congestion, adverse weather, road blockades, power grid disruptions, or unforeseen mechanical failures beyond reasonable control.

7. Limitation of Liability

Our liability is strictly limited to replacing non-conforming batch volumes verified prior to setting. Namma Concrete is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential structural damages resulting from improper placement, vibration, curing, or site formwork failures.
